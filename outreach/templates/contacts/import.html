How columns are matched

Headers are matched case-insensitively. Recognised: email, first name, last name, name (split automatically), title / job title, phone, linkedin, company / organization, domain / website, industry, employees / company size, tags, source, timezone, owner. Every other column becomes a custom field you can use in templates — e.g. a pain_point column becomes {{ '{{pain_point}}' }}.

Invalid emails are skipped. Addresses on the suppression list are imported but will never be emailed.
